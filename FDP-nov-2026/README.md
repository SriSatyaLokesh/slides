# Faculty Development Program (FDP) — November 2026

Presentations and course materials delivered during the Faculty Development Program (FDP) on Generative AI, AI-assisted engineering, and Autonomous AI Agents.

---

## Overview

This program explores the evolution of AI tools from interactive conversational assistants to autonomous agents capable of complex workflow execution. The sessions provide educators, researchers, and developers with actionable techniques for integrating AI into technical education, research workflows, and modern software development pipelines.

## Key Highlights

- **Developer Productivity & AI Pair Programming:** Practical strategies for leveraging AI assistants in daily coding, documentation, and debugging.
- **Agentic Architectures:** Transitioning from simple completion models to goal-oriented agents with tool-calling capabilities and autonomous decision cycles.
- **Workflow Automation:** Building end-to-end multi-step pipelines combining structured reasoning and external APIs.
- **Classroom & Lab Applications:** Incorporating agentic workflows and AI-driven development into computer science and engineering curriculum.
